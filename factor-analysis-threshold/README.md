## The one hypothesis

`DSSExample18Q`, defined in `lean/FreeDiagonal/Variety/Example18.lean`:

> Over ℚ, every polynomial in the off-diagonal variables ψ_ij (i < j) that lies in the ideal
> M_{8,3} generated by the 4 × 4 minors of the generic symmetric 8 × 8 matrix (its diagonal entries
> being further variables) has a power in the ideal generated by the off-diagonal 4 × 4 minors and
> the septads.

This is what [DSS07, Example 18] computes. It is an explicit argument of `paper_v4_theorem_6_Q` and
`paper_v4_theorem_6_variety_Q` and of nothing else; the passage from ℚ to the real ideal I_{8,3}
and to complex points is proved in Lean. Two further front-door statements are about the
hypothesis itself: `paper_v4_example18_of_generators` (a generating set each member of which lies in
J_{8,3} or has its square there, which is the form in which [DSS07] report the result, gives
`DSSExample18Q`), and `paper_v4_example18_nonvacuous`.

The computation is reproduced in [`example18-singular/`](example18-singular/).
